Date of research: 2026-10-24. Raw pages: (raw pages not kept).
Confidence: PRIMARY = vendor page fetched 2026-10-24. SECONDARY = search summary or third party. UNKNOWN = not verified.
Column "Verbatim quote" is copied from the fetched text; quotes are at most 25 words. Where a column is inferred from a table layout, the row says so.

Method notes:

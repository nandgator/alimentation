Gotchas:

- The idle thresholds are 7-day 95th-percentile values. A quiet Postgres host can meet all three, which means it can qualify as idle. (Implication from the rule, not a vendor statement.)
- Over-limit A1 instances are disabled, then deleted after 30 days, unless the account is upgraded. Source: https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/FreeTier/freetier.htm
- "the Always Free status is not transferable to another resource." Source: https://www.oracle.com/cloud/free/faq/ (a deleted free resource frees capacity; the status does not move).
- Block-volume minimum is 47 GB in one passage and the default is 50 GB in another. Use 50 GB for planning.
- SECONDARY (forum, not authoritative): users report reclaimed instances despite active use, and an initial charge at a card-verification upgrade. Source: https://community.oracle.com/customerconnect/discussion/664398/oci-has-reclaimed-idle-not-always-free-compute-resources
- SECONDARY (one forum anecdote): A1 "out of host capacity" in Hyderabad. Not an official statement.

# Hosting fit scout 1: Appwrite, Firebase, Convex

As of 2026-10-24. Scope: rubric R1 to R10 for the three platforms in the brief.

Method: curl only. Every page cited was fetched on 2026-10-24; raw files are in (raw pages not kept). No search-result summaries were used, so no SECONDARY sources appear. "PRIMARY" means a vendor page or vendor doc index fetched 2026-10-24. "UNKNOWN" means not found in fetched primary sources; nothing was guessed.

Gotchas

- A Free project freezes at any resource limit: "Your project will freeze, and Appwrite Console will continue running in read-only mode." (https://appwrite.io/pricing)
- "You cannot purchase additional resources under the Free plan." (https://appwrite.io/docs/advanced/billing/free.md)
- Free per-project caps are enforced by disabling the oldest resources first (for example, databases beyond the first).
- Self-managed backups are Pro-and-above only: "Self-managed backups are available for Pro plans and above." (https://appwrite.io/docs/advanced/security/backups.md)
- Managed PostgreSQL needs a paid plan and a payment method: "Native databases are available on paid plans, and a payment method must be attached to your organization." (https://appwrite.io/docs/products/databases/postgresql)
- Fair use: "Exceeding these limits may lead to throttling or suspension of services" (https://appwrite.io/docs/advanced/billing/fair-use-policy.md)
- Indian cards: RBI verification for up to $150 applies to add-on usage on paid plans (https://appwrite.io/docs/advanced/platform/billing).
- Free numeric quotas (requests, bandwidth, storage, function time): not in the fetched text, so UNKNOWN.

- Spark Firestore free-tier values: 1 GiB, 50,000 per day, 20,000 per day, 20,000 per day. Row labels were not captured in extraction, so I cannot confirm which value is reads, writes or deletes. (https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/quotas)

- "linking a Cloud Billing account to your project will auto-upgrade your project to the Blaze pricing plan" (https://firebase.google.com/docs/projects/billing/firebase-pricing-plans)
- Automatic downgrade from Blaze to Spark occurs in listed situations; the list was not read (UNKNOWN).
- SQL Connect / Cloud SQL trial: if not on Blaze by trial end the instance is archived, then deleted 90 days later (https://firebase.google.com/pricing).
- The SQL Connect rows "No-cost up to 10 GiB/month" and "No-cost up to 250K operations per month" appear on the pricing page, but the row-to-product mapping in the extracted text is ambiguous.
- Deleted projects count toward the project quota until fully deleted, which takes 30 days (https://firebase.google.com/docs/projects/learn-more).

Gotchas

- "Free has hard resource caps, while Starter can exceed its included resource amounts with usage-based pricing." (https://docs.convex.dev/production/state/limits.md). What happens on Free when a cap is hit: not read (UNKNOWN).
- Free included usage: 1,000,000 function calls per month and 20 GB-hours of action compute per month (https://docs.convex.dev/production/state/limits.md).
- "On the Free/Starter plan, up to two backups can stored per deployment at a time." (https://docs.convex.dev/database/backup-restore.md; wording as published)

- Non-US regions bill at 1.3x (https://docs.convex.dev/production/regions.md).

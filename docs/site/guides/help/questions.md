# Questions

## Do I log every meal?

No. Everyone is billed for the day's meal unless they say otherwise. You log only exceptions: leave, a switched dish, extras.

## Why is my bill in italics?

Because it is a **projection**. The month has not ended, so the days still to come are counted at the meal the menu says. It turns into the actual bill when the month ends.

## I forgot to log a leave. Is it too late?

Not until the month is closed. You can add it for a day that has passed. It will show as **Late**, with the number of days, so the supplier can see when it was added. If the month is closed, ask the admin.

## Why does the form say "late"?

It is telling you, before you press **Add**, that the day has gone. It is not a refusal. It is so nobody can say later that they did not know the entry would show.

## The supplier has not confirmed my payment. What now?

Wait, and nothing is lost. A pending claim never counts as paid and never counts against you, and **Payments** shows how long it has waited. The supplier can decide it any time, even after the month has closed.

## Can the admin change my entries?

The admin runs the books, so yes, they can add and remove entries. They cannot do it quietly: it is recorded with their role, and you can see it in your **History**: _"The admin removed your Leave entry for 14-Oct-2026."_

## Can the supplier confirm a payment I did not make?

The supplier decides what they received. If they confirm money that never arrived, it is recorded under their role and visible to you.

## Can another member see my days or my bills?

No. The database does not return another member's rows, whatever is asked for.

## I made a mistake in a claim.

While it is **Pending**, **Withdraw** it and file a new one. After the supplier has decided, only the admin can reverse the decision, with a reason.

## Why can't I change a day in an earlier month?

That month has been **closed**. Closing freezes meals and leave. Ask the admin; a change they make is flagged as made after the close, with the difference.

## Does a leave day cost anything?

Not by default. Some kitchens charge a share of the meal for a leave day. **Rates & rules** says what yours does.

## What is the "Acting as" switch?

You hold two roles, usually admin and supplier, because you run the kitchen and cook. The switch chooses which one you are using right now.

## Can I change the theme?

Yes. **Theme** has three choices: **Auto** follows your phone or computer, **Light** and **Dark** stay as you set them. On a wide screen it is at the foot of the menu on the left; on a phone it is under **More**. The home page and these guides have it too, and one choice applies to all of them.

## Can I use it without a connection?

You can read the last statement you opened. Adding entries and claims needs a connection.

## What happens when the kitchen outgrows the free tier?

The meter warns at 70 and 90 percent. At 100 percent there are 14 days before new writes are refused, and reading and copying are never blocked. You can [move](/guides/fair/your-data/) with one action, and nothing is lost.

## Who can see the notes I write?

You, the supplier and the admin. They are encrypted in the database.

## Can I get my data out?

Yes, at any time, even when the kitchen is over its limits: **Your data**, then **Download everything**.

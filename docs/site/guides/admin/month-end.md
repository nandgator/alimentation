# Month end and catching up

A kitchen runs on people who are sometimes unavailable. A supplier is ill, a member is away for a week, someone forgets to log a leave. Alimentation gives everyone **until the month is closed** to put that right, and then freezes what is billed.

The aim is two things at once: nobody can quietly change a bill, and nobody is locked out for being busy.

## The timeline

| When                    | What can happen                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **During the month**    | Members log exceptions. Claims are made and decided. Days that have already passed can still be added, marked late. |
| **The month ends**      | The bill stops being a projection and becomes **actual**. Nothing is frozen yet.                                    |
| **The catch-up window** | Until the month is closed, anyone can still add a late entry, and the supplier can still decide claims.             |
| **The close**           | The admin closes the month (or the kitchen's rules close it by themselves). **Meals, leave and extras freeze.**     |
| **After the close**     | Claims, decisions and settlements carry on. A change to a meal needs the admin and is flagged.                      |

## How long is the catch-up window?

It is up to your kitchen, in **Rates & rules**, under **Month close**:

| Setting                 | The window                                                                                            |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Closed by the admin** | Open until the admin closes it. The default.                                                          |
| **Closes by itself**    | A fixed number of days after the month ends, say five, so everyone knows the deadline.                |
| **Never closed**        | There is no freeze at all: any month can change at any time. Suits a kitchen that is happy with that. |

A fixed window is kinder to everyone. The member knows the date, the supplier knows the date, and the admin never has to chase.

## What each person does

**Members.** Before the month is closed, add anything you forgot. It is allowed after the day; it will show as late. After the close, ask the admin.

**The supplier.** Decide the claims waiting for you. If you were away, decide them now: it works at any time.

**The admin.** Open **Month close** and read **Still waiting**:

- **Claimed, not decided**: a member says they paid and the supplier has not answered yet. Closing does not touch it.
- **Query open**: a payment was disputed. Resolve it with a settlement.

Then press **Close**, for one person or for everyone.

## When someone was away

**The supplier was unavailable.** Claims stay pending. They never count as paid until the supplier confirms, and they never count _against_ the member. Nothing is charged for waiting, and it all still works after the close. If the supplier cannot come back, the admin can add someone else as a supplier.

**A member was away and did not log.** They add the days before the close, marked late. After the close they ask the admin, who adds them. That change is flagged as made after the close, and shows by how much the bill would have changed.

**The admin was away.** Set the month to **Closes by itself**. It closes on schedule, at the amounts it had, with no one needed.

## After the close

- The **billed** amount is fixed. A later change to a meal does not alter it. It shows as **Changed after close** with the difference.
- Whether the admin may still change a closed month at all is the kitchen's choice: **Allowed, and flagged** (the default) or **Not allowed**.
- The admin can **reopen** a month. Reopening is recorded, and the member sees _"The admin reopened your Oct-26 bill."_

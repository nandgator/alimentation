# Running the books as the admin

You set the kitchen up and keep it straight. Everything you do is recorded with your role and the time, and the people it affects can see it.

## People

**People** lists everyone in the kitchen. The name in each row opens the person. There you change their name, contact and dates, and **disable** them or **enable** them again.

- **Invite** on a member's row makes a one-use link for seven days, tied to the email you type.
- **Disabling** someone keeps their history and stops new entries. It is not deleting.
- **Eating from** and **Until** are the dates a member is billed between.
- **Contact** is yours alone, and is stored encrypted.

## Rates and rules

- **Rate cards** carry the prices and the weekly menu from a date. They are **added, never edited**: a new card applies from its date, and earlier months keep their prices.
- **Rules for this kitchen** are the options in [the rules reference](/guides/admin/kitchen-rules/). A change applies from the first of a month. It can start in the current month or later, never in a month that has passed.

## Holidays

A holiday is **confirmed** (the meal is cancelled) or **tentative** (kept on the list, not counted). Changing one changes the bills for that day in any month that is not closed.

## Logging for someone else

The admin can add an entry for a member, for example when they phone to say they are away. It is stored with your name as the author, and the member sees it in their **History** as _"The admin logged Leave on 14-Oct-2026 for you."_ If it is after the day, it is marked late.

## Settlements

**Settlements** are your own entries on a member's bill: **Payment** (money taken outside the app), **Query** and **Query Resolved**. They are **added and never changed**. The member and the supplier can both see them.

> [!TIP]
> Record a payment **once**. Either the member claims it and the supplier confirms it, or you record a settlement. If both happen, the bill counts two payments; the way to put that right is to reverse the confirmed claim, with a reason.

## Reversing a decision

When the supplier confirmed or disputed a claim by mistake, open **Payments**, find the claim, and use **Reverse**. You give a reason. The original decision stays on record, the claim no longer counts, and the member sees the reversal and the reason. The member files a new claim.

## Closing the month

**Month close** freezes what each person is billed for a month that has ended. It shows, beside each name, what is **still waiting**: money claimed but not yet decided, and open queries. Closing does not stop those: payments carry on. Read [Month end and catching up](/guides/admin/month-end/) before your first close.

## Audit

**Audit** is the raw record of every change in the kitchen, written by the database: what, who and when. Names and notes appear as "(encrypted)". Members and the supplier have their own plain-language **History**.

## Your data

**Your data** shows the free-tier meter and lets you take a copy, move the kitchen, or delete it. See [Moving and deleting](/guides/fair/your-data/).

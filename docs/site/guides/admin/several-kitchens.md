# Running more than one kitchen

If you run one kitchen, none of this is shown to you, and nothing about your kitchen is different.
If you run two or more, there are three small things to help.

## Your kitchens, with numbers

The page that lists your kitchens shows, beside each one, what is owed to it this month, how many
payments are waiting for you to confirm, and whether it still needs setting up. Above them is one
line across all of them. You do not have to open each kitchen to see where you stand.

## Copy a setup

A second kitchen usually has the same menu, prices, rules and holidays as the first. Under your
kitchens there is a box for copying them. You choose the kitchen to copy from, the kitchens to copy
to, and what to copy (menu, prices, rules, holidays from this month on). The menu is copied on its
own. Prices go only between kitchens that both track money; to or from a kitchen without money they
are left out, and the box says so.

It shows you what would be added **before** anything is, and says what each kitchen already has.
Nothing a kitchen already has is changed, and a kitchen still says no to anything its own rules
refuse, such as a menu that would change a month that is closed.

## A head office

If your kitchens belong to a group, the group's head office can see how each is doing. It sees the
totals for the month: how many people are fed, how many meals, what was billed, what was paid, what
is owed, and how many payments wait. **It never sees a name.** It does not see who owes what, who
was on leave, or any note, and it does not become a member of your kitchen.

- **Your kitchen joins only if you say so.** The head office makes a link and sends it to you. In
  **Your data** you paste it, see whose it is, and choose to share. If you run both the kitchen and
  the head office, you can add the kitchen from the head office's page.
- **Everyone in your kitchen is told.** The dashboard says who receives the totals.
- **You can stop whenever you like**, from **Your data**. The head office can stop receiving them
  too.

See [Your data](/guides/fair/your-data/) for what else is yours to take, move or delete.

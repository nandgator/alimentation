# Days off, and being away

## Days the kitchen is off

If you do not cook on a working day, open **My log** and add **Supplier Off** for that date. It applies to **everyone** and removes that day's meal from every bill. Every member sees _"The supplier marked the kitchen off on..."_ in their history.

If you add it after the day, it is marked **late**, like any other late entry. After the month is closed, only the admin can change it.

## If you are away

Payments that need your decision wait. They are not lost, nobody is charged for waiting, and the member sees how long it has been. You can decide them when you are back, **even after the month has been closed**, because closing freezes meals and leave and never payments.

If you will be away a long time, ask the admin to add someone else as a supplier, so there is always a person who can decide.

## If you cannot come back

The admin can add someone else as a supplier, and the claims still waiting can be decided by them. The admin cannot confirm a payment for you: only a supplier decides.

# Confirming payments

You cook, and you are the one who knows whether the money arrived. Alimentation gives you the queue of payments to decide.

## What you see

Your **Dashboard** shows every member's bill for the month: meals, leave, the bill, what is paid, the balance and a status, with the total **receivable** at the foot. You can read every member's entries and claims, because the bills depend on them. You cannot change a member's entries.

## To confirm

**Payments** opens with **To confirm**: the claims nobody has decided yet. For each one you see who, the month, the amount, how it was paid, the day, the reference, and **how long it has been waiting**.

- **Confirm** when you have received it. The amount counts as paid and lowers that member's balance.
- **Dispute** when you have not, or the amount is wrong. A **reason is required**. A dispute opens a _query_ on that member's bill, which the admin settles.

> [!WARNING]
> **You decide each claim once, and the first decision is final.** There is no edit and no delete. If you made a mistake, tell the admin: only the admin can reverse a decision, they must give a reason, and the reversal is recorded and shown to the member. The member then files a new claim.

Check that the money arrived before you press **Confirm**. A confirmation is your word to the member, and it is recorded under your role.

## If you are also the admin

You see a switch, **Acting as**, in the side menu. Choose **Supplier** to confirm or dispute a claim, and **Admin** for everything else. The two roles are kept apart on purpose: as the admin you cannot confirm a payment.

## History

**History** shows what has been done to the entries, claims and bills you can see, and by whom, newest first. It cannot be changed.

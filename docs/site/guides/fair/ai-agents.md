# AI helpers

Some people like to have software act for them: log a leave when they say so, or tell them what they
owe. A kitchen can let that happen, and it is built so that it can never go further than the person
who allows it.

## What a helper is here

A helper gets a **key** from a person. It acts **as that person**, in **one** of the roles they have
in **one** kitchen, and for as long as the key lasts (ninety days unless the person chose otherwise,
a year at most). The person can end the key at any moment, and it stops that moment. The key is
shown once, when it is made, and the kitchen keeps no copy of it.

## What it may do

A helper can **read** what its person can read. If the person allows it, it can also **add and
withdraw entries** (leaves, extras, days off), and **say that a payment was made**. That is the
whole list.

An assistant that uses the kitchen's MCP server is also offered a few starting points, such as "log
my meals for a day", "what is my balance?" and "say a payment was made". They are offered only if
the key can do everything they involve, and each tells the assistant to look first, to say what it
is about to do, and to ask you before it writes anything.

## What it may not

A helper can never confirm or dispute a payment, close or reopen a month, change who is in the
kitchen, change the prices or the rules, take a copy of the kitchen, move it or delete it. Those
stay with a person. It also cannot do anything its person could not: if the kitchen's rules say an
entry is too late, they say so to the helper too, and a member's helper sees only that member's own
bills.

## How you can tell

What a helper does is written in the kitchen's own history, in the same place as everything else,
and it says so:

> You, through the agent “Lunch bot”, logged Leave on 28-Oct-2026.

Everyone a change is about can read that line, just as they can read changes anyone else made. If it
was done in your name and you did not expect it, tell the admin: they can end the key.

For how a person makes a key, and what the helper is told, see
[AI agents and MCP](/docs/api/agents/).

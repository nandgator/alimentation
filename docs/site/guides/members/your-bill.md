# Your bill and your days

You eat at the kitchen. Alimentation shows you what you owe and how it adds up. You do not need to do anything for a normal day: **you are billed for the day's meal unless you say otherwise.**

## Your dashboard

The dashboard opens on the current month. The three large figures are the ones that matter:

- **Bill**: what you owe for the month.
- **Paid**: what has been confirmed as paid.
- **Balance**: the bill less what you have paid.

Below them are **Actual to date** (the part of the bill for days already gone), **Claimed** (money you have said you paid that the supplier has not yet decided on), **Earlier unpaid** (what is left from earlier months) and your **Status**.

> [!NOTE]
> A figure in _italics_ is a **projection**: the month has not ended, so the days still to come are counted at the meal the menu says. It becomes **actual** when the month ends.

## Daily

**Daily** lists each working day with the meal, any extras and the cost. Days that have not happened yet are greyed. If a day is wrong, say so under [telling the kitchen](/guides/members/telling-the-kitchen/).

## Why a bill moves

Your bill changes when:

- you log an exception (leave, a switch, extras);
- the supplier marks the kitchen off for a day;
- the admin adds or changes a holiday;
- the kitchen's prices change from a date.

Each of these is written down with who did it and when, and you can see them in your [History](/guides/members/history/). How the sum is done, with a month worked through to the rupee, is in [How a bill is worked out](/guides/how/bills/).

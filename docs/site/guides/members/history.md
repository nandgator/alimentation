# History, and using it with no signal

## History

**History** lists everything that has been done to your entries, claims and bills, newest first, and by whom:

- _You claimed ₹1,000 for Oct-26 (UPI)._
- _The supplier confirmed your claim of ₹1,000 for Oct-26._
- _The admin removed your Leave entry for 14-Oct-2026._
- _It was added 3 days after the day itself._

It shows only your own records. It names you, and the roles of the people who acted ("The admin", "The supplier"), never another member. It cannot be changed or cleared. This is how you can tell that nobody has quietly changed your bill.

## No signal

The last statement you opened is kept on your phone, so you can read it with no signal. You will see a note that it is the saved copy. Adding entries and claims needs a connection.

## Putting it on your phone

In your browser's menu, choose **Add to home screen** (or **Install**). It then opens like any other app.

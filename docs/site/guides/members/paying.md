# Paying, and saying you have paid

You pay the supplier yourself, by UPI, cash or bank transfer. Then you tell Alimentation, so the kitchen's books agree. This is called a **claim**.

## Making a claim

On **Payments**:

1. Enter the **amount**, the **month** it is for, how you paid (**UPI**, **Cash**, **Bank transfer** or **Other**), the day you paid, and a reference if you have one.
2. Press **Claim payment**. It shows as **Pending**, and your bill shows it under **Claimed**.
3. The supplier **confirms** it, or **disputes** it with a reason. A confirmed payment lowers your balance. A disputed one opens a query that the admin settles.

A part payment is its own claim.

## The statuses

| Status        | Meaning                                                                           |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pending**   | Waiting for the supplier. It shows how long it has waited.                        |
| **Confirmed** | The supplier says they received it. It counts as paid.                            |
| **Disputed**  | The supplier says it is not right, and said why. The admin settles it.            |
| **Reversed**  | The admin undid a decision, with a reason. It no longer counts. File a new claim. |

## A mistake

While a claim is **Pending**, **Withdraw** takes it back. Once the supplier has decided, the decision is final: only the admin can reverse it, with a reason, and you will see both.

## If the supplier is away

> [!TIP]
> Your claim waits, and **Payments** tells you for how long. Nothing is lost and nothing is charged for waiting. A pending claim never counts as paid, and it never counts against you. It is decided when they are back, even after the month has closed.

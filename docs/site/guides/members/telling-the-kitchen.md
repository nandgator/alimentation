# Telling the kitchen you will not eat

Open **My log**, pick the date and the change. You only log what is different from the day's meal.

| Change                                | What it does                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Leave**                             | No meal that day, and no charge (unless your kitchen charges part of a leave day). |
| **Switch to Veg / Switch to Non Veg** | You get the other meal that day, at its price.                                     |
| **Extra Chapati / Extra Bhakri**      | Extra pieces, one to twenty, at the price per piece.                               |

You can add a short note. It is stored encrypted, and is visible to you, the supplier and the admin.

The form tells you what is wrong **before** you press **Add**. The rules it follows:

- Only working days. A day your kitchen does not cook is refused.
- One leave, and one switch of each kind, per day.
- Only for yourself, and only on days you are billed.

## Taking an entry back

In **My log**, **Remove** takes back an entry of yours, while the month is still open. Removing is written down too, and shows in your [History](/guides/members/history/).

## Adding something after its day

People forget, and people travel. You are allowed to add a leave, a switch or an extra for a day that has already passed, until the month is closed. The form says so first, and the entry is marked **Late**, with the number of days. Everyone who can see it sees that it was added late. Nothing is hidden, nothing is refused, and nobody has to take your word that you were away.

If your kitchen has set a **cut-off** (for example, "by 8 pm the day before"), the form will refuse an entry after it, and tell you to ask the admin. The admin can still add it for you. It is then marked late and shows that the admin added it.

## If the month is closed

Once the month is closed you cannot change it. Ask the admin. A change they make is flagged as made after the close, and shows by how much your bill would have changed. See [Month end and catching up](/guides/admin/month-end/).

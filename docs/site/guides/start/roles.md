# Who can do what

A kitchen has three roles. Each person has one, except the owner of a small kitchen, who is often the admin and the supplier at once.

| Role         | Who it is                                                      |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Member**   | Someone who eats at the kitchen and is billed for it.          |
| **Supplier** | The person who cooks, and who confirms or disputes payments.   |
| **Admin**    | The person who runs the books: prices, people, rules, closing. |

## The table

| Action                                             | Member | Supplier |          Admin           |
| -------------------------------------------------- | :----: | :------: | :----------------------: |
| See their own bill, days and claims                |  Yes   |   Yes    |           Yes            |
| See everyone's bills and claims                    |   No   |   Yes    |           Yes            |
| Log leave, a switch or extras **for themselves**   |  Yes   |    No    |            -             |
| Log leave, a switch or extras **for someone else** |   No   |    No    |  Yes, marked as theirs   |
| Mark the kitchen off for a day                     |   No   |   Yes    |           Yes            |
| Claim a payment                                    |  Yes   |    No    |    Yes, for a member     |
| Confirm or dispute a claim                         |   No   |   Yes    |            No            |
| Reverse a decision, with a reason                  |   No   |    No    |           Yes            |
| Record a settlement (a payment, a query)           |   No   |    No    |           Yes            |
| Change prices, rules, holidays and people          |   No   |    No    |           Yes            |
| Close or reopen a month                            |   No   |    No    |           Yes            |
| Read the **History** of their own records          |  Yes   |   Yes    | Reads the full **Audit** |
| Move, copy or delete the kitchen                   |   No   |    No    |           Yes            |

Two rules sit under the table and do not bend:

- **A member sees only themselves.** Another member's days, claims and bills are not hidden by a screen; the database does not return them. This holds through the app and through its interface.
- **Only the supplier decides a payment, and only the admin reverses one.** The admin cannot confirm a payment for the supplier.

## One person, two roles

In a kitchen where the owner cooks, one sign-in holds both the _Admin_ and _Supplier_ roles. A switch at the top of the side menu, **Acting as**, chooses which one you are using. It remembers your choice on that device.

> [!WARNING]
> When one person is both admin and supplier, nobody else checks them. The members' protection in that case is that everything the owner does is written down and visible to the member it affects. See [Honest by design](/guides/fair/honest-by-design/).

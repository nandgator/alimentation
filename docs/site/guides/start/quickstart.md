# Set up a kitchen in ten minutes

This is for whoever runs the kitchen's books: the **admin**. You need a sign-in, a list of what a meal costs, and the names of the people you feed.

## 1. Sign in and start a kitchen

Sign in with the account your kitchen's host has set up (often Google). On **Your kitchens**, fill in **Start a kitchen**:

| Field                      | What to put                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Name**                   | What your people call it, for example "YMCA mess".                                                                |
| **Address**                | Letters, numbers and dashes. It becomes the link: `/w/ymca-mess`. It is chosen once.                              |
| **I am also the supplier** | Leave this ticked if you cook, or if you are the one who confirms payments. You can switch between the two roles. |

You land on the dashboard. It tells you what is missing: **Add a rate card to start billing**, then **Add the people you feed**.

## 2. Add a rate card

Open **Rates & rules** and choose **New rate card**.

| Field                               | Meaning                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Applies from**                    | The first day these prices are used. Earlier days keep the prices they had.                |
| **Veg meal**, **Non-veg meal**      | The price of one meal.                                                                     |
| **Extra chapati**, **Extra bhakri** | The price of one extra piece, beyond the default portion.                                  |
| **Extras charged monthly**          | A flat amount for the things that are not counted one by one (papad, salad), once a month. |
| **Menu on Mon... Fri**              | Which meal is served each working day. Everyone gets this meal unless they say otherwise.  |

> [!TIP]
> A rate card is never edited. When prices change, add a new card with a new start date. Past months keep the prices they were billed at.

## 3. Add the people

Open **People** and choose **Add a person**.

- **Name** and **Role**: _Member_ (someone you feed), _Supplier_ (the person who cooks and confirms payments) or _Admin_.
- **Contact** is optional. Only you can see it, and it is stored encrypted.
- **Eating from** and **Until** are the dates a member is billed between. Leave **Until** empty while they are still eating.

If you ticked **I am also the supplier**, you already appear twice in the list, once as _Admin_ and once as _Supplier_. That is one sign-in holding two roles.

## 4. Invite them

On a person's row, press **Invite**, type their email address, and send them the link it makes.

- The link works **once**, for **seven days**, and **only with the email you typed**.
- They sign in with that email and are in. Nobody else can use the link.

## 5. Look at the rules

**Rates & rules** also shows the rules your kitchen runs on. The defaults suit most kitchens:

- meals Monday to Friday; only confirmed holidays cancel a meal;
- a leave day costs nothing;
- the monthly extras apply from the first meal;
- you, the admin, close each month;
- a member claims a payment and the supplier confirms it;
- an exception can be added at any time; one added after its day is marked late.

Each of these can be changed from the first of a month. See [the rules](/guides/admin/kitchen-rules/).

## 6. Add the holidays

Open **Holidays** and add the days the kitchen does not cook. A holiday is either **confirmed**, which cancels the meal, or **tentative**, which is kept for later and does not. You can confirm one when it is certain.

## 7. Watch the first month

Members see their own bill the moment they are invited. The first bill is a **projection** (shown in italics) until the month has ended, and becomes **actual** once it has. When the month is over, see [Month end and catching up](/guides/admin/month-end/).
